Getting Started

What to Expect in the First Week of Home Care

Starting home care can feel emotional for everyone involved. Knowing what the first week typically looks like reduces anxiety and helps the relationship begin on solid ground.

Before the first visit

After the free consultation we prepare a care plan that reflects routines, preferences, and any clinical notes. You will know who is coming, when, and what they will focus on.

The first visits

Early visits are often about relationship as much as tasks. A good caregiver observes how your loved one likes things done, introduces themselves calmly, and works at a pace that feels respectful rather than rushed.

How families can help the adjustment

  • Be present for the first couple of visits if possible
  • Share preferences, history, and what helps your loved one feel secure
  • Give feedback early — small adjustments are easiest in the first week
  • Allow a little time for trust to form; consistency is the biggest driver of comfort

Most families tell us the house feels calmer once a reliable rhythm is established. We stay in close contact during the first period so any concerns can be addressed quickly.

Every family situation is different. Book a free consultation and we'll help you understand the options that fit yours.
