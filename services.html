Our Services

Care built around your family, not a package.

Whether your loved one needs a few hours of companionship a week or round-the-clock nursing support, we build a plan around their actual routine — and yours.

In-Home Care Services

Personalized, compassionate support that helps seniors stay independent in the comfort of their own home.

Personal Care Assistance

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting — delivered with patience and respect for dignity and independence.

Medication Management

Reminders, dosage tracking, and communication with pharmacies and physicians to keep prescriptions on schedule.

Companionship & Social Support

Genuine conversation and shared activities that ease loneliness and support emotional well-being.

Light Housekeeping & Meal Prep

Everyday household tasks and nutritious meals aligned with dietary needs and preferences.

Mobility & Transfer Assistance

Safe, supported movement around the home to reduce the risk of falls and injury.

Respite Care for Families

A dependable break for family caregivers, with the same standard of care continuing in your absence.

Higher-acuity needs

Skilled nursing, at home

For families managing more complex conditions — post-surgical recovery, chronic disease management, or dementia care — our Registered Nurses and Licensed Practical Nurses provide clinical support alongside compassionate daily care.

We coordinate directly with your physician and family so care stays consistent as needs change.

Healthcare Staffing Solutions

Reliable, credentialed staffing for long-term care facilities and healthcare organizations across BC and Alberta.

Registered Nurses & RPNs

Skilled clinical staff for facilities managing higher-acuity resident needs.

Licensed Practical Nurses

Dependable LPN coverage for day-to-day resident care and continuity.

Health Care Assistants

Compassionate HCAs supporting daily living needs and resident well-being.

Cooks & Kitchen Staff

Culinary staff who understand dietary requirements in care environments.

Housekeeping

Facility-trained housekeeping staff maintaining safe, hygienic living spaces.

Emergency & Contract Staffing

Fast response for urgent gaps in scheduling, including rural and remote communities.
