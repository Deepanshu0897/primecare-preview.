Common questions

Answers to what families ask us most before getting started.

What's the difference between an HCA, LPN, and RN?

A Health Care Assistant (HCA) supports daily living needs like bathing, dressing, and companionship. A Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) can also manage medications and basic clinical tasks. A Registered Nurse (RN) handles more complex clinical needs, such as wound care or chronic condition management. We match the right level to your situation during your free consultation.

How much does in-home care cost in BC?

Cost depends on the type of care, hours needed, and caregiver credentials required. Because every family's situation is different, we provide a clear, no-obligation quote after your free consultation rather than a generic price list.

Do you provide the same caregiver for every visit?

Whenever possible, yes — we prioritize matching your family with a consistent, familiar caregiver rather than rotating staff, because trust and routine matter for both seniors and families.

How quickly can care start?

In many cases we can begin care within a few days of your consultation, sooner for urgent situations. Timing depends on the specific caregiver skills your situation requires.

Is in-home care covered by insurance or government funding in BC?

Some costs may be partially covered through extended health benefits or provincial home support programs, depending on your situation and assessed care needs. We're happy to help you understand your options during your consultation.

Which areas of BC do you serve?

We serve 19 communities across Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley, Vancouver Island, the BC Interior, and the Sunshine Coast — including Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, Langley, Coquitlam, Abbotsford, Victoria, and Kelowna.
