About Prime Care Services

Built on a simple belief: seniors deserve to stay home.

Our story

Prime Care Services was founded with a straightforward goal: help seniors across British Columbia get the support they need without leaving the home they love. What started as a small team of nurses and care assistants has grown into a province-wide network — but the approach hasn't changed.

We're a team of Registered Nurses, Licensed Practical Nurses, and Health Care Assistants who believe good care starts with actually knowing the person you're caring for — their routines, their preferences, their family.

What guides our work

Dignity first

Every task, from mobility support to conversation, is approached with respect for independence.

Real continuity

We prioritize matching the same caregiver to your family whenever possible.

Clear communication

Families and physicians stay informed as care needs evolve — no surprises.

Licensed & accountable

Every caregiver is credentialed, background-checked, and trained to provincial standards.

Our team

Our care team includes Registered Nurses, Licensed Practical Nurses, Health Care Assistants, cooks, and housekeeping staff — all driven by the same mission: fostering meaningful connections and creating a nurturing environment where seniors can thrive.

We also work closely with long-term care facilities across BC, providing dependable staffing so residents receive consistent, quality care.

Want to talk with our team?

We're happy to answer questions about care options, staffing, or anything else on your mind.
