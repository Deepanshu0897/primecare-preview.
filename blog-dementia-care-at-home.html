Dementia Support

Keeping a Parent with Dementia Safe at Home

Many families want to keep a parent with dementia at home for as long as it is safe and sustainable. With the right supports, this is often possible longer than people expect.

Safety first, dignity always

Simple environmental changes make a large difference: consistent lighting, reduced clutter, secure exits if wandering is a risk, clear labelling, and routines that feel familiar. Professional caregivers who understand dementia can reinforce these routines rather than fight them.

The value of consistent faces

People living with dementia often respond better to familiar caregivers. Rotating staff increases confusion. We prioritize matching the same caregiver whenever possible so trust can build and daily care feels predictable.

What caregivers can help with

  • Personal care delivered with patience and respect
  • Medication reminders and observation for changes
  • Meaningful engagement and redirection when needed
  • Support for family members who need respite

Every dementia journey is unique. A free consultation lets us understand the specific stage, behaviours, and family capacity before recommending a plan.
